HOW TO PLAY

🎮Move: WASD / Arrow Keys on desktop. Tap D-Pad buttons or swipe on mobile. Cannot reverse direction.
⌨️Keys: P = Pause/Resume · R = Restart · Space = Start/Restart · D-Pad = directional buttons on screen
🥦Vegetables: +5 pts, grow 1 segment. Cyan glow. Most common. Safe and reliable.
🍎Fruits: +15 pts, grow 2. Grants a 3-second speed burst. Orange border activates.
💎Diamonds: +50 pts, grow 3. Activates POWER MODE — electric blue snake for 10 seconds. Rare!
🛡️Shield Power-up: Pick up to survive your NEXT wall hit. One-time save shown by red dashed ring on snake head.
🧲Magnet Power-up: Any food within 3 cells auto-snaps to you for 8 seconds. Yellow border shows it's active.
❄️Slow-Mo Power-up: Cuts speed ~40% for 6 seconds. Invaluable at Legend level!
👻Ghost Power-up: Pass through WALLS for 5 seconds — walls wrap instead of killing you. Purple glow. Stacks with Wrap Mode!
🌀Wrap Mode (button): Walls become portals — snake exits the opposite wall. Toggle anytime.
🔥Combo: Eat 3+ items in 5s = ×2 · 5+ = ×3 · 7+ = ×5! Chain power-ups for mega streaks.
🏆Levels: Rookie (0) → Hunter (50) → Warrior (150) → Master (300) → Legend (500). Speed rises each level.
💡Pro: Use U-shapes and spirals. Grab Shield before diamonds. Magnet + Wrap = insane streaks.
