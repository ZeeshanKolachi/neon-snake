HOW TO PLAY
🚀
OBJECTIVE
Guide your snake through space to collect the glowing planet food that appears on the canvas. Every planet you eat makes your snake grow longer and adds to your score. The game ends when your snake hits a wall — so plan your path carefully as the snake grows. Survive as long as possible, collect power-ups, and climb the Hall of Fame leaderboard!

PRO TIPS
  • You cannot reverse direction — plan moves in advance
  • The longer the snake, the less open space you have
  • Grab Wall Shield before making risky corner moves
  • Stack 2× Score with Hard difficulty for maximum points
  • Slow Motion is great when the snake gets dangerously long
  • Power-ups disappear after 9 sec — prioritise them
  • Your top 10 scores are saved across sessions

MATH CHALLENGE MODE
Enable the 📐 ENABLE MATH CHALLENGES toggle before launching.

A math question appears in a green bar above the game canvas. The correct answer is shown as text on the planet food. Eat the planet to confirm the answer — a toast shows the full equation.

Easy → single-digit addition (e.g. 3 + 7 = ?)
Medium → multiplication (e.g. 6 × 9 = ?)
Hard → larger multiplication (e.g. 13 × 11 = ?)
